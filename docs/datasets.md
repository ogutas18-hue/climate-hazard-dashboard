### **Datasets**

#### **Administrative Boundaries**



Source: Kenya County Boundaries



Layer: Homa Bay County Boundary



Format: GeoJSON



Purpose: Used to assess accessibility and support visualization of transportation networks in climate hazard monitoring and decision-support applications.



#### **Settlements**

Source: OpenStreetMap



Layer: Homa Bay Settlements



Format: GeoJSON



Purpose: Used to identify populated locations potentially exposed to climate hazards and support risk visualization within the dashboard.
