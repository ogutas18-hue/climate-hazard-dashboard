### **Dashboard Methodology**

#### **Purpose**

To develop an interactive climate hazard monitoring dashboard that supports environmental monitoring, risk visualization, and early warning decision-making.



#### **Data Sources**

##### Administrative Boundaries

* Kenya County Boundaries



##### Roads

* OpenStreetMap



##### Settlements

* OpenStreetMap



##### Vegetation Condition

* Sentinel-2 NDVI derived from Google Earth Engine



#### **Processing Workflow**

1. Extract Homa Bay County boundary.
2. Acquire transportation and settlement datasets.
3. Generate NDVI from Sentinel-2 imagery.
4. Classify vegetation condition into risk categories.
5. Publish layers to ArcGIS Online.
6. Develop dashboard for visualization and decision support.



#### **Dashboard Components**

* Interactive Web Map
* Mean NDVI Indicator
* Maximum NDVI Indicator
* Vegetation Status Indicator
* Climate Risk Summary



#### **Applications**

* Climate Monitoring
* Early Warning Systems
* Environmental Assessment
* Disaster Risk Reduction
* Decision Support
